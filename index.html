ABOUT

Hi, I'm Joseph Marshall, an Audio Production and Music Technology graduate from the University of Chichester, having completed a BA (Hons) with Upper Second-Class Honours (2:1). During my studies, I developed a particular interest in audio post-production and video game audio, whilst also enjoying opportunities to work across a range of audio disciplines. Outside of university, I've always enjoyed exploring audio beyond the classroom, from acoustics and instrument design to experimenting with different forms of media production. I'm naturally curious about the way sound works and enjoy learning about new techniques, technologies, and creative approaches.

I've always been fascinated by sound and naturally find myself exploring the questions and ideas it raises. What has always drawn me most to audio is its ability to immerse an audience and completely transform visual media. Whether it's a carefully mixed piece of music, impactful sound design, or convincing spatial audio, I'm fascinated by how the right sound can shape emotion, atmosphere, and storytelling. Creating that level of immersion is what I find most rewarding.
